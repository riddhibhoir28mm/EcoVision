🫁 AIR QUALITY

How Poor Air Quality Can Affect Us

🫁

Respiratory System

Exposure to polluted air can irritate the respiratory system and make breathing more difficult.

❤️

Heart & Circulation

Long-term exposure to air pollution can affect cardiovascular health.

🧠

Overall Well-being

Poor environmental conditions can affect quality of life and daily well-being.

💧 WATER QUALITY

Why Clean Water Matters

Clean water is essential for people, animals, agriculture and ecosystems.

🚰 Safe Drinking Water Clean water supports healthy communities.
🐟 Aquatic Ecosystems Healthy water supports aquatic organisms.
🌾 Agriculture Water quality is important for crops and soil.
🌍 Environmental Balance Healthy water systems support biodiversity.

⚠️ REMEMBER

Prevention Is Better Than Pollution

Check environmental conditions, reduce pollution wherever possible and make sustainable choices.
