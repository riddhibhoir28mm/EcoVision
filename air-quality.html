🌫️ UNDERSTANDING AIR POLLUTION

What Are We Breathing?

Air quality is influenced by pollutants released from vehicles, industries, construction, burning and natural sources. Monitoring these pollutants helps us understand environmental conditions.

PM2.5 Fine particles that can penetrate deep into the lungs.
PM10 Larger airborne particles such as dust and pollen.
NO₂ Commonly associated with vehicle and combustion emissions.
O₃ Ground-level ozone formed through atmospheric reactions.
