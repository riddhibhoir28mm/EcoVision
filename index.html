UNDERSTAND THE NUMBER

What does AQI mean?

The Air Quality Index converts air pollution measurements into an easy-to-understand scale.

0–50 Good
51–100 Moderate
101–150 Sensitive Groups
151–200 Unhealthy
201–300 Very Unhealthy
301+ Hazardous
