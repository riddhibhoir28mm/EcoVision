🌍 ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS

Understand. Act. Protect.

Learn how air and water pollution affect our health, environment and future.

KNOW THE PROBLEM

Two Major Environmental Challenges

🌫️

Air Pollution

Air pollution occurs when harmful gases and particles enter the atmosphere.

Common sources include vehicle emissions, industrial activities and burning of fuels.

💧

Water Pollution

Water pollution occurs when harmful substances enter rivers, lakes and other water sources.

Common sources include waste disposal, industrial waste and untreated wastewater.

⚠️ COMMON CAUSES

Where Does Pollution Come From?

Human activities and poor environmental practices can contribute significantly to pollution.

🚗 Vehicle Emissions Exhaust gases and particles
🏭 Industrial Activities Emissions and industrial waste
🗑️ Improper Waste Disposal Waste entering land and water
🌳 Deforestation Loss of natural environmental protection

❤️ WHY IT MATTERS

Effects of Pollution

🫁

Human Health

Poor environmental quality can negatively affect human health.

🐟

Aquatic Life

Water pollution can damage aquatic ecosystems.

🌱

Ecosystems

Pollution can disturb natural ecosystems and biodiversity.

🌎

Future Generations

Environmental protection helps preserve resources for the future.

🌱 TAKE ACTION

Small Actions. Big Difference.

Reduce waste, conserve water, use cleaner transportation and help protect nature.
