🌱 ECO TIPS

Small Changes. Cleaner Future.

Simple everyday actions can help protect our air, water and environment.

🌍 EVERYDAY ACTIONS

What Can You Do?

🚲

Choose Cleaner Transport

Walk, cycle or use public transport whenever possible.

Fewer vehicle emissions can help reduce air pollution.

💧

Save Water

Turn off taps when water is not needed and avoid unnecessary wastage.

Conserving water helps protect valuable freshwater resources.

♻️

Reduce Waste

Reuse items and separate recyclable materials from other waste.

Reducing waste can decrease pollution and pressure on landfills.

🌳

Protect Green Spaces

Protect trees, plants and local green spaces.

Green spaces support biodiversity and contribute to healthier surroundings.

🌎 ECO CHALLENGE

Start With One Change Today

Choose one eco-friendly habit and make it part of your daily routine.
